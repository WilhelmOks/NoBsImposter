## Description (English)

No BS. Seriously.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No subscription fees. No sign-up, no account, no tracking. Completely free — just the game and nothing else.

No BS Imposter is a party game for your group. Everyone shares one phone and gets the same secret word — except the imposter, who doesn't. Give one-word clues, listen closely, and figure out who's bluffing. The imposter wins by blending in.

How to play:
1. Pass the phone around — each player holds the card to secretly see their word.
2. One player is the imposter and never sees the word.
3. Take turns describing your word without giving it away.
4. Discuss, vote, and unmask the imposter.

Features:
• 100% free, forever — no ads, no purchases, no subscriptions, no account
• Works completely offline
• 2,000+ words per language across 10 categories (Celebrities, Movies, Animals, Food & Drink, Countries, Sports, Jobs, Everyday Objects, Instruments, Landmarks)
• English and German
• 3–12 players, pass-and-play on a single device
• Pick your categories, name your players, and set how many imposters
• Optional: tell the imposter the category — or leave them completely in the dark
• Clean, modern design that gets out of your way

Grab a few friends, pass the phone, and find the imposter.
